Our Mandate

Section 5 of Executive Order No. 111, Series of 2025 provides that the Provincial Community Defense (PCD) Action Center shall perform functions in data, information, and communications management; incident management; operational coordination; resource management and mobilization; and support systems, including geographic and other space-based information, as well as the promotion of public-private partnerships to strengthen community defense initiatives.

Welcome to the TALAMDAN Geospatial Portal!

TALAMDAN stands for the Technologically Advanced Lens for the Assessment and Management of Development and Actions Needed. It is a user-friendly geospatial database and Geographic Information System (GIS) hub. managed by the Provincial Planning and Development Office (PPDO).

Access to this website is restricted to the Iloilo Provincial Capitol local network; please visit https://191.168.54.133/offices.html while connected to the Capitol's internal Wi-Fi or LAN.

IPGFMS

What is IPGFMS?

It is the centralized digital backbone of the provincial capitol, acting as a comprehensive data repository that connects all IPG offices. Moving beyond the scope of standard accounting software, it is designed as a 'living archive' that documents the province’s administrative life and history.

PROJECT BRIEFERS

Under the leadership of Governor Arthur R. Defensor, Jr., the Province of Iloilo has championed transformative and innovative programs that advance resilient, inclusive, and sustainable development. Guided by a vision of a robust, progressive, globally competitive, and resilient Province of Iloilo, the Provincial Government has invested and collaborated with National Government Agencies in advancing development projects that translate into accessible and efficient public services. Through decisive leadership, sound policies, and collaborative governance, these initiatives continue to enhance service delivery, empower communities toward a safer, more progressive, and prosperous province.

DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS

The Province of Iloilo's MoRProGRes (Movement for a Robust, Progressive, Globally Competitive, and Resilient Iloilo) serves as the guiding framework for advancing inclusive and sustainable development across the province. Through this vision, the Provincial Government has implemented strategic infrastructure projects that not only improve connectivity and public facilities but also strengthen resilience, expand access to essential services, and enhance the quality of life of Ilonggos.
